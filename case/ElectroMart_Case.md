# ElectroMart: Working Capital Policy

ElectroMart is an electronics retailer with about $10 million a year in sales. Sales are steady, but the company keeps borrowing on its bank line of credit to carry its inventory and receivables. The CFO wants to free up cash without raising new money from outside the company. There are three levers to pull:

1. Carry less inventory.
2. Collect from customers faster.
3. Take longer to pay suppliers.

ElectroMart pulled all three. Below are its financial statements before and after the changes. To isolate the working capital changes, assume sales and operating costs are the same in both periods and ignore any earnings retained between the two balance sheets. Treat each balance sheet as ElectroMart's typical, year-round balances.

## Balance sheets

| | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash | 300,000 | 500,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 900,000 | 800,000 |
| Inventory | 1,200,000 | 600,000 |
| **Total current assets** | **2,400,000** | **1,900,000** |
| Net fixed and other assets | 2,500,000 | 2,500,000 |
| **Total assets** | **4,900,000** | **4,400,000** |
| | | |
| Accounts payable | 600,000 | 900,000 |
| Notes payable (bank line of credit) | 1,200,000 | 400,000 |
| **Total current liabilities** | **1,800,000** | **1,300,000** |
| Long-term debt | 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Common equity | 2,100,000 | 2,100,000 |
| **Total liabilities and equity** | **4,900,000** | **4,400,000** |

## Income statement (same in both periods)

| | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Revenue | 10,000,000 |
| Cost of goods sold (COGS) | 6,000,000 |
| Gross profit | 4,000,000 |
| Operating expenses | 2,500,000 |
| **Operating income (EBIT)** | **1,500,000** |

## Other information

- Use a 365-day year.
- All of ElectroMart's sales are on credit.
- The bank line of credit charges 8% a year.
- Suppliers sell on terms of 2/10, net 30. ElectroMart gets a 2% discount if it pays within 10 days. Otherwise the full amount is due in 30 days. Assume all purchases are on these terms and that purchases equal COGS.

## The CFO's questions

1. How long is a dollar tied up in ElectroMart's operations before and after the changes? (Use the cash conversion cycle.)
2. How much cash did each lever free up, and where did that cash go?
3. Was this a good change in working capital policy? What risks did ElectroMart take on?
